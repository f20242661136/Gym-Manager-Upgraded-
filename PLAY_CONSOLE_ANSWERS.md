## Store copy

### Short description
Manage members, fees, attendance, renewals and daily gym operations in one app.

### Full description
Gym Manager helps gym owners and staff manage everyday gym operations from one organized workspace.

Keep member records up to date, track memberships and renewals, record membership fees, manage attendance, and review day-to-day gym activity from your phone.

**Member management**
Add and manage members, contact details, member photos, membership status and renewal information.

**Memberships and renewals**
Create membership plans, track active and expired memberships, handle renewals, discounts and admission fees, and keep membership history organized.

**Fees and payment records**
Record membership fees and payment methods such as cash, bank transfer and supported local payment methods. Review payment history for each member.

**Attendance**
Check members in and out from the front desk and use member QR codes for faster attendance workflows.

**Dashboard**
View useful gym information including members, attendance, payments and other operational activity.

**Offline-friendly member management**
Member changes can remain available during temporary connectivity interruptions and synchronize when the connection returns.

**Built for gym operations**
Gym Manager is designed for gym owners and authorized staff who need a practical system for front-desk and member administration.

Features available to each user may depend on their gym role and configuration.
