What Gym Manager does

Gym Manager is a business administration app used by gym owners and authorized staff to manage members, memberships, attendance, fee records and related gym operations.

Information processed

  • Account information such as email address and account identifiers.
  • Gym/business information.
  • Member name, phone number, optional email address and profile photo.
  • Optional member age, gender and body measurements such as weight, height, chest, waist and calculated BMI.
  • Membership plans, dates, status and renewal information.
  • Attendance and check-in/check-out records.
  • Membership fee/payment records such as amount, payment method, reference and notes. Gym Manager does not require card numbers or banking passwords.
  • Local notification settings and locally scheduled reminders.
  • Crash/diagnostic information only if production diagnostic reporting is enabled.
Information about minors: Gym Manager is designed for adult gym owners and staff, not for children. A gym may enter member records for a minor. The gym operator is responsible for obtaining any consent or authorization required by applicable law.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate users, operate gym/member management, manage memberships and renewals, record attendance and fees, synchronize permitted offline changes, deliver reminders, secure the service and support troubleshooting.
