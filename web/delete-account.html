Delete your Gym Manager account

You can request deletion of your Gym Manager account and associated personal data.

From the app

Open Gym Manager → Settings → Privacy & account → Request account deletion.

Without the app

Send the request from the email address associated with your Gym Manager account. This page must remain available without requiring users to reinstall or sign into the app.

Request account deletion

What happens next

  • We verify that the requester controls the account.
  • We delete the app account and associated data that is not required to be retained.
  • If the requester is the only owner of a gym workspace, ownership/business-record handling must be resolved before destructive deletion.
  • Any information retained for a legitimate legal/security reason is handled as described in the Privacy Policy.
